# Objective
One owner for "what state is this campaign in" and "is this ad running" (feature Contract L; audit W7, decisions 6 and 12;
triage #1, #16; drill-11 R4 H3／G3, E5, N3; planning #19). Today three definitions of "running" (`ad_status.is_closed`,
`== "ACTIVE"` ×4, a TS 4-word map) and ended／closed／imported collapse into "not closed", so a disapproved ad holds capacity,
ended campaigns read as live and imported ones are `draft` forever.

# Done

   root, today)`, `record(conn, org, brand, campaign_id)`, `campaign_ids(conn, org, brand, states)`, `brands(conn, states)`,
   `monitored(campaign, today)` with `ENDED_GRACE_DAYS = 7`, `LABELS`, `can_close`. No platform literal: delivery goes through
   `adapters.delivery(platform, native_status)` → `meta_read.STATUS_TABLE` (the table in Contract L). `ad_status.py` is deleted.
