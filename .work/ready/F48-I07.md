# Objective
One money calculator (feature Contract M, decision 8, R5 B2／N1, planning #1–#3, #17): `app/ads/budget.py` turns a total into
daily budgets and back, every money figure rounds the same way, adopting rounds the total, nothing re-splits on read, the
campaign cannot change its period or product after adoption, a new total after adoption re-splits the ad sets and says
so, and the month-portion figure names its basis.

# Done
1. `budget.whole(value)` (half-up, `floor(x + 0.5)`; `None` passes through), `budget.daily_of(total, days)` (`floor(total /
   days)`, `None` when days ≤ 0), `budget.total_of(daily_sum, days)` (exact product, `None` when either side is unknown);
   `_round_money` and every `round(` on money in `budget.py` use `whole`. `learning_rank.whole` and `wizard_options.py:89`
   delegate to `budget.whole` (keep `learning_rank.whole`'s formatted-string return as a wrapper). Scenario rows: `daily =

   (→ `over_cap == 0` by construction). `repository._budget_total` = `total_of(Σ daily, days)` (no `round(…, 2)`).

   transaction (`repository.set_budget_planned` or an existing writer) — adopt rounds the total. After adopt
