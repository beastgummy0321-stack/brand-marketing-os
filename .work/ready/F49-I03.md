# Objective
"Which days were read" has one answer that survives several ranges finishing in any order and is clipped to the campaign
(feature Contract V; audit W9; triage #10; drill-11 R3 C3-D6, G1). Today coverage is the last finished task's range: a backfill
erases the last 30 days' proof, days flap between 缺 and read, and every campaign younger than 28 days raises 資料不足.

# Done
1. `app/ads/coverage.py` per Contract V: `day_states`, `window(campaign, cutoff, days)` (ends at `min(cutoff, end_date)`, starts
   no earlier than `start_date`), `missing_days`, `covered`, `gap`. Rows prove a day (`data`); a clean recorded range proves an
   empty day (`empty`); anything else is `missing`. Monthly-budget rows (`is_monthly_budget_row`) never count.
2. `external_repository.record_sync` keeps `observations.ranges` as the merged union (adjacent／overlapping ranges merge; a
   failed attempt keeps the ranges, as it keeps `last_success_at` today); the legacy `coverage` key is read as one range and no
   longer written. `external_sync` records the clean task range into the union; `simulated.extend_coverage` does the same.
3. Readers move to the module: `triage` (`_coverage`／`_missing_days`／`_window_covered`／`coverage_gap` delegate or go; the judged
   windows and `_account_cutoff`'s end use `coverage.window`, so an ended campaign is judged up to its end date),
   `outcome`, `home_overview._missing_days`, `workspace_reads._totals`, `ab_pairs`, `change_timeline`, `period_grades`,
   `review_evidence`, `connections` (campaign-level reads only; the account monthly proof is unchanged), `closeout._coverage_check`.
   Triage `data` serves `days [{day, state}]` for the judged window; `data.coverage` keeps its `{start, end}` shape (first and
   last read day inside the judged window, null when none) so today's screens keep working until F49-I10; `lib/adsTriageApi.ts`
   gains `days`.
4. Machine check in `test_ads_one_ruler.py`: a read of `observations`' `coverage`／`ranges` key outside `coverage.py` and its two
   writers (`external_repository.record_sync`, `adapters/simulated.extend_coverage`) fails; the monthly task's own `coverage`／
   `last_covered` reads in `external_sync`／`month_budget`／`connections` are allowlisted by name.
5. Tests (`test_ads_coverage.py`): two disjoint clean ranges completing in either order leave both covered; a day with rows and no
   recorded range is `data`; a cleanly read day with no row is `empty`; a failed read keeps earlier ranges; a 10-day campaign
   judged on day 12 has no missing day before its start; an ended campaign's window ends at its end date. Existing expectations
   that a 28-day window before `start_date` is 缺 change to the clipped window.
