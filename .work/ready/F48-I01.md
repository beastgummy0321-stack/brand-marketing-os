# Objective

cost, the sample gate becomes a shown flag, `too_new` replaces `below_sample_gate`, and `GET …/rules` serves every
threshold, label and glossary sentence from the two ruler modules. Consumers (cleanup, settle, closeout, month, A/B, pause
reasons) are F48-I02; keep them untouched here except for the rename they need to compile.

   0 results otherwise → `("insufficient", TOO_NEW_LABEL, "too_new")`; unknown window → 資料不足 with the window's reason; a
