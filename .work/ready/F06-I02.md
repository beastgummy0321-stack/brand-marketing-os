# Objective

投放結構 gets the strategy page's shape (ARCHITECTURE.md §6.2 decision 16; the user: 「做的像前面策略那步
一樣，下放超大按鈕給使用者快樂路徑」). A newcomer sees what the system built and is told they need not
change it. They then press one full-width button: 「開始做素材 →」 hands the ads to 素材工作檯 and lands on
素材; with no ad at all, 「新增廣告 →」 opens the existing add dialog. Everything an expert edits moves into
a closed 「調整結構（進階）」. 投放結構 stays on the newcomer path (removing it is an explicit non-goal).
Reference image: `docs/design/ads/05-strategy.png`.
