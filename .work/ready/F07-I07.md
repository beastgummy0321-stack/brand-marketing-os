# Objective

One image-prompt round becomes one model call that writes exactly 10 complete natural-language prompts
in the industry-agnostic craft grammar, with each slot's look assigned by the program, the brand's
derived identity and the look texture appended, and each prompt stored as its own version row with a
fingerprint and a recommendation. Rules: ARCHITECTURE.md §5.7–§5.10; F07 Decisions 9–18, 20–23, 25.

Read first, in full: `.work/features/F07.md` Decisions; ARCHITECTURE.md §5.7–§5.10. The department
prompt source is outside your worktree (read-only):
`D:\0UserProfile\Desktop\marketing\.harness\scratch\probes\image-prompt-v4\task_v4.md` and
`run_v4.py::generate` (worksheet order). Out of scope: the read side (F07-I08), styles (F07-I09).
Never delete or overwrite anything under `data/`.
