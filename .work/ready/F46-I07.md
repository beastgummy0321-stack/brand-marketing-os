# Objective
After a closeout the owner sees what was learned and can plan the next period from it, and the strategy says which learnings it
used (feature outcome 4; drill-10 L5, L6 front half, D5). Layout spec = `.harness/scratch/prototype/f46-learnings/index.html`
screens `#/close` (confirm → 結案中… → result) and `#/strategy` (「查看策略依據」 panel); open them and click through.
Data: `POST …/close` as today, then `GET …/coordinate-learnings?campaign_id=` (Contract L); `basis.ranked_learnings` (Contract L
shape) on the campaign's strategy basis; month summary suggestions with kind `next_plan`.

1. `CloseCampaignDialog`: the confirm sentence as the prototype (「結案後會把這期 N 則廣告照每筆成本排名、判定留下或換掉，寫進品牌智庫「投放學到的」。
   活動之後鎖定，不能再改。」, N = the campaign's planned ads; early-close and coverage-gap parts unchanged). After a successful close
   the dialog stays open on the result: 「已結案：留下 N 則、換掉 M 則」, `rule_text`, the compact `LearningsTable` rows, and 關閉 /

   failed learnings read after a successful close says 「已結案，學到的暫時讀不到」 with the two links still there; an empty view
   shows its `empty_reason`. The parent's close callback runs when the dialog closes, not before.
