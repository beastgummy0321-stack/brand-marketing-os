# Objective
One clock answers "which day is it for the user" and every instant comes from `db.now_utc()` (feature Contract K; audit W11;
intake #15, #18 window length, other F5 backend half, triage #12 machine-clock half). Today 54 calls ask the machine
(`date.today()`, `datetime.now(UTC)`), so between 00:00 and 08:00 Taipei the inbox, 本月 numbers, the orders window, SKU cost
dates and the AI monthly limit are yesterday's or last month's, and the drill clock cannot move them.

   org_id, zone=None)` (None → `today_for_org - 1`, resolved through the module attribute), `org_day_start`, `org_month_start`,
   `day_window(end, days)` (exactly `days` dates). Docstring states the "which clock for which question" table.
