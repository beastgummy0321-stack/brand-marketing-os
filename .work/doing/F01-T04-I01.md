# Objective

Rebuild the page shell to layout C. The work view inside it is `F01-T04-I02`; this issue leaves
`BriefDetail.tsx` mounted as it is.

Settled in ARCHITECTURE.md §5.1-§5.3. The layout is the existing `.workspace-split`
(`docs/design-system.md` §5), not a new pattern.

# Done

- Left rail (`.workspace-subnav`), three groups, always all visible, in this order:
  1. **開始生成** - four entries 文案／圖片提示詞／影片腳本／自由生成, each a `<button>`. Selecting one
     opens the new-brief form in the work area with that part pre-ticked.
  2. **派工任務** - three source groups in this order: 檔期 (`source_kind === "schedule_node"`),
     網站, 品牌內容. The last two have no source_kind yet and therefore always render their empty
     state. Every group with no brief shows exactly 「尚無任務」.
  3. **工單** - 全部 and 已採用. 全部 lists every brief of this brand whatever its state, queued and
     failed included; 已採用 filters to briefs with an adopted round
     (`parts[*].adopted_round !== null`). Closed briefs are not dropped: inside 全部 they sit in a
     collapsed 「已關閉」 section at the bottom, so today's close / 重新開啟 flow stays reachable. The
     list call becomes `fetchBriefs(brandId, "all")` and the split on `closed_at` happens in the
     component. When 工單 has no brief it reads 「還沒有工單，從上面的開始生成建立一張。」 - not
     「尚無任務」, which belongs to 派工任務 only.
- The page header keeps `PageHeader` titled 「素材工作檯」 and loses the 「新工單」 button: 開始生成 is
  the only way in to the form. No remaining string in `frontend/src/components/workbench/` points
  the user at a 右上角新工單 button (today `BriefList.tsx:49/51`, `WorkbenchPage.tsx:165`).
- The work area shows the new-brief form when nothing is selected, and the selected brief otherwise.
  The `?brief=` search-param selection, the 10s list / 2s detail polling and the background-refresh
  rule in today's `WorkbenchPage.tsx` are kept as they are.
- The new-brief form replaces `NewBriefPanel`'s side panel and lives in the work area: parts as a
  multi-select (the four names), a copy-length picker (短版／中版／長版 with `LENGTH_HINTS`) shown when
  文案 is ticked, per-part count fields shown when 圖片提示詞 (1-30) or 影片腳本 (1-5) is ticked, an
  optional product, and free text. It posts `request.parts` / `request.copy_length` /
  `request.image_prompt_count` / `request.video_script_count` / `request.free_text` /
  `request.use_brand_knowledge`. The seven coordinates and the on-image-text fields are gone from
  this form - coordinates come only from a schedule node, and the backend dropped `on_image_texts`
  from the contract in `F01-T01-I01`.
- `workbenchApi.ts`: `NewBrief` drops `kind`, `coordinates`, `visual_tone`, `visual_concept`;
  `NewBriefRequest` (workbenchApi.ts:25-31) gains `parts: string[]` and
  `copy_length: "short"|"medium"|"long"`. `on_image_texts` is **not** removed here: the same type is
  also the read-back shape of `BriefDetail.request` (workbenchApi.ts:79) and its last reader is
  `BriefDetail.tsx:263`, which this issue must not touch - make the key optional
  (`on_image_texts?: string[] | null`), stop sending it from the new form, and let `F01-T04-I02`
  delete key and reader together.
- `copy.ts`: `KIND_LABELS` **stays exactly as it is** - it labels `workbench_brief.kind`, which is
  still a display label after `F01-T01-I01` (including `ad_creative` → 排程節點), and it is read by
  `BriefList.tsx:29/108` and by `BriefDetail.tsx:693`, which this issue does not touch. Add
  `PART_LABELS` (文案／圖片提示詞／影片腳本／自由生成) for the part multi-select, and a `SOURCE_LABELS`
  map (檔期／網站／品牌內容); `BUTTON_LABELS` gains 「開始生成」 and the empty-state string 「尚無任務」.
  `IMAGE_PROMPT_ADVISORY` stays.
- `frontend/e2e/smoke.spec.ts`: the workbench step keeps line 61 (heading 「素材工作檯」) and replaces
  line 62 (the removed 「新工單」 button) with exactly these assertions - the smoke brand is brand-new
