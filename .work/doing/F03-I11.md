# Objective

Money spent finds its campaign without any day-splitting rule. §0: it serves the **outcome** link;
its input is the platform's own daily rows keyed by the entity this campaign is bound to; its
output is the 成效 tab's numbers and the input to 結案; its trace is `source` on every outcome row
(`meta` or `simulated`) and the day it belongs to; it is **core**.

# Done

- `app/ads/outcome.py`:
  - `ingest_meta(conn, org_id, brand_id, campaign_id, *, start, end)` — reads
    `app/meta/repository.py::daily_entity_spend` once per level (`adset` for groups, `ad` for ads)
    for the brand's bound accounts, matches rows by `external_id`, and upserts `ads_outcome_daily`
    with `source='meta'`. Spend that matches no bound entity is **not** invented into the campaign;
    it simply is not this campaign's. 重疊分帳 does not exist here: a bound entity belongs to
    exactly one campaign, and if two campaigns claim the same `external_id` the ingest refuses and
    says which two (that is a binding bug, not a splitting rule).
  - `purchases` and `revenue` come from what the meta row already carries (`roas` × spend gives
    revenue; purchases from the actions the existing pull stores). Where a value is missing the
    column stays `NULL` and every reader treats `NULL` as 未追蹤 — never `0`.
  - `summary(conn, …, campaign_id) -> dict` — the 成效 payload: totals (spend, purchases, revenue,
    CPA, ROAS conservative **and** optimistic, CTR), a daily series for the chart, a per-group and
    per-ad table with each row's spend share, and `coverage` (how many ad-days have no row) plus
    `last_data_at`. Every number carries the window and the basis it was computed on; nothing is
    computed twice in two places.
  - `sample_bucket(purchases, days) -> "低"|"中"|"高"` (低 < 10 purchases or < 3 days; 中 10–49;
    高 ≥ 50) — the one place the bucket is defined, used later by suggestions (F04) and by the
    learning level (F03-I12). **Never a percentage.**
- `app/ads/routes.py`: `GET .../campaigns/{id}/performance` (summary) and
  `POST .../campaigns/{id}/ingest`, which **dispatches on how the campaign was published**: a
  Meta-published campaign pulls what the daily Meta job already stored (no new network call, no new
  scheduled job in F03 — the 6-hour cadence and the refresh button are F04); a simulated campaign
  calls the simulated adapter's `generate_outcomes` to bring it up to today. One endpoint, so the
  成效 tab has one action regardless of adapter. `adsApi.ts` gains the types and the call.
- This issue is sequenced after F03-I08 and F03-I10 because all three write `app/ads/routes.py`,
  `app/ads/repository.py` and `adsApi.ts`; append your slice, do not re-shape theirs.
- `app/tests/test_ads_outcome.py` (the falsifier): spend on a bound entity lands on the right
  campaign day; spend on an unbound entity lands nowhere; two campaigns bound to the same external
  id makes the ingest refuse with both names; a day with no data returns `NULL`, and the summary
  reports 未追蹤 rather than `0`; conservative and optimistic ROAS are both present; the sample
  bucket boundaries (9/10, 49/50, 2 days/3 days) are exact; the ingest endpoint on a simulated
  campaign adds the missing days and on a Meta campaign does not call the simulator.

# Verify

`uv run python -m pytest app/tests/test_ads_outcome.py -q` — the unbound-spend case and the
NULL-is-not-zero case are the two that keep the numbers honest.

# Blocked if

`daily_entity_spend` does not return purchases in a usable form (it returns spend, impressions,
clicks, roas, currency). Then purchases stay `NULL` for Meta-sourced rows in F03, CPA is reported as
算不出來 for those rows, and you report it — do not add a field to `app/meta/**` in this issue and
do not compute purchases from ROAS and an assumed order value.
