# Objective
The ads screens pick and print dates only through the org clock, and a deferral is an org calendar date (feature Contract K;
audit W11; frontend-ads F5; other F5 ads half; drill-11 controls c3-05 「M/D 再看」, c3-06). Today 40 ads files build `Date`s:
「7 天後」 is seven days from the laptop's clock, a custom date parses as UTC midnight, `revisit_at` and every 「M/D」／「HH:MM」 stamp
are printed in the browser's zone, and day lists are computed with `toISOString().slice(0, 10)`.

   here. No `Date` leaves the module.
