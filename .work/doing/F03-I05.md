# Objective

One recommended strategy, with its reasons and its unknowns, and the loop's memory in it. §0: it
serves the **strategy** link; its input is the draft campaign, the brand facts with their update
times, today's effective product prices, the learnings from `learning_ledger` and the program's
budget scenarios; its output is the strategy payload the plan issue turns into rows; its trace is
the `llm_task` row plus 查看策略依據 listing exactly which rows went into the prompt; it is **core**.

# Done

- `app/departments/ads/strategy.md` — one department task file. It is given: the goal sentence, the
  objective and success criterion, the dates, the brand facts (each with 更新時間), the products
  with today's effective price (a stale price arrives labelled 價格可能過期), the learnings
  (`list_learnings`, each with its level and whether it 需要重新驗證), and the program-computed
  budget scenarios. It returns JSON only: one `recommended` strategy — `{allocation: [{role, label,
  pct, rationale}], why: [...], unknowns: [...], alternatives: []}` — where `role` is one of
  `new` / `retarget` / `explore`. **One recommendation**; `alternatives` stays empty unless there is
  a real trade-off, and then each carries what it trades. The file states that the model never
  invents a number: percentages and reasons only, amounts come from the program.
- `app/ads/strategy.py` — `generate(conn, org_id, brand_id, campaign_id, *, actor)`:
  assembles the context, calls `run_task` (`task_type="ads_strategy"`), applies
  `budget.scenarios()` and `budget.split()` to turn the percentages into amounts, and persists the
  result on the campaign (`budget_basis_json` = the scenarios + basis, strategy JSON stored in the
  campaign row — no new table). The recommended scenario is the default; the user's chosen scenario
  and any hand-typed total are stored by the same function via
  `select_scenario(conn, …, scenario_key, custom_total)`, which writes `budget_planned` and
  `budget_scenario`. `budget_planned` is a record of what was adopted — after 採用策略 the group
  daily budgets are the truth (F03.md 「One source of truth for money」) and nothing rewrites
  `budget_planned` from them.
- `app/ads/repository.py` — `budget_history(conn, brand_id) -> dict | None`, the aggregate
  `budget.scenarios()` takes as `history` (shape and rules: F03.md 「The budget engine's `history`
  input is pre-aggregated by its caller」). `None` when the brand has no settled spend — that is what
  makes the strategy tab show 冷啟動估算 rather than a number. Never return a dict without `cpa`.
- `app/ads/budget.py` — **one string fix only**, no behaviour change: the history captions must read
  「上一期日預算 NT$<n> ×0.6／×1／×1.6」, not 「近 30 天 CPA 中位數 x …」 (the median CPA is context in
  `basis`, not a factor in that multiplication). Add to `app/tests/test_ads_budget.py` the falsifier
  that a `history` without `cpa` produces no caption mentioning a CPA median. Do not touch
  `breakeven_cpa` / `target_cpa` / `split` / the cold-start branch — they are reviewed and merged.
- **查看策略依據**: the strategy payload carries `basis = {knowledge_item_ids, llm_task_id,
  input_context_hash, brand_facts: [{id, label, updated_at}], prices: [{product_id, price,
  effective_from, stale}], learnings: [{id, text, level, needs_revalidation}], estimated_fields}`
  — `estimated_fields` names which numbers are 推估／冷啟動估算 so the screen can label them
  without guessing. Read `llm_task.knowledge_item_ids_json` / `input_context_hash` as the existing
  schedule prompt path already does.
- `app/ads/routes.py` — `POST /api/ads/brands/{b}/campaigns/{id}/strategy` (generate) and
  `POST .../strategy/scenario` (pick 最低／推薦／積極 or a custom total). Both return the campaign
  detail. `frontend/src/lib/adsApi.ts` gains the types and the two calls.
- `app/tests/test_ads_strategy.py` (the falsifier): with a fake provider returning percentages,
  the persisted allocation amounts sum exactly to the chosen scenario total; a brand with one
  learning has that learning's text inside the prompt sent to the provider **and** in
  `basis.learnings`; a brand with none produces a basis whose `learnings` is empty and no
  fabricated sentence; a stale price is marked stale in the basis; a provider failure returns a
  readable error and leaves the campaign in `draft` with no half-written strategy.
