   computed with `triage.grade_window(view, spend, cap_value, days)` over the campaign window, where `cap_value =
   targets.resolve(...)["value"]` and `view`/`spend` are the same aggregate the totals come from (no second aggregation); with no

   bound ads, joined in Python (never per item).
3. `app/ads/slots.py::_slot` — gains `"format_key"` (the ad's `format_key`, `None` for an ad without one); every `_slot(...)` call site
   passes it. Docstring sentence.

   (one per entry of the existing `owners` map, `name` from the entity row, money as float, ordered by commitment desc) so the
   approve page can print 「9 月活動還有 5 天沒跑完，每天 1,500」. `view()` passes it through unchanged.
5. Tests: `test_ads_outcome.py` — a summary with cap and spend/results → `totals.grade` matches `grade_window`'s answer; no cap →
