# Objective
The page whose job is "say why there is no judgement" says it for every reason, from the backend's table, offers the exit that
fixes that reason, and paints only days that were read (feature Contracts Q, O, V, L; frontend-ads F8; triage #7; drill-11 R3
C3-D6, B7, R4 H4／Q2). Today it has rows for two of seven reason codes, says 「資料齊了」 after a failed read, paints the 7 days before
`coverage.end` as read, offers 重新檢查 on a draft that is not on any platform (「平台照常投放」 hard-coded), and has no 結案 exit for
an ended campaign.

# Done
1. Reason rows: one row per `reason_code` present, text from `useAdsRules().reasons[code]` (`title`, `text`); the exit follows
   `fix` — `resync` → 重新檢查, `connections` → the data connections page, `cap` → the campaign's strategy tab, `wait`／null → no
   button. `reasonText`／`REASON_ORDER` go (rows follow the order the codes are served; while rules load, rows print no words —
   no fallback literal).
2. Read state: a `failed`／`auth_expired`／`disconnected` sync shows `data.status.label` and `data.sync.error` as the hero (never
   「資料齊了」 after a failed read); 重新檢查 posts the campaign sync and follows `useSyncOutcome({brandId, campaignId})`, printing
   its `text` (found／empty／partial／failed with reason) — no `syncJob`／`job.status`／`job.error` here.
3. Days: the tiles paint `data.days` (`data` 有資料, `empty` 讀過・沒花費, `missing` 缺) with the served window's dates as the
   heading; `addDaysLocal`／`lastDays`／`shownWindow` go (no local date arithmetic for read days).
4. Lifecycle: `lifecycle.state === "draft"` → hero 「這個活動還沒上架，沒有平台資料可讀」 with an exit to the workspace, no
   重新檢查 and no platform chip; the platform chip prints only for a live campaign; `ended` → a forward exit 「結案這一期 →」 to the
   closeout link (`?tab=perf&close=1`).
5. `oneRuler.test.ts`: delete every allowlist entry that names F49-I10.
6. Tests: each of the seven codes renders its served row and exit (fixture rules carry `reasons`); a failed sync shows its label
   and error, not 資料齊了; a partial re-read prints its reason; `empty` and `missing` days render differently; a draft shows the
   not-launched hero without 重新檢查; an ended campaign shows the 結案 exit.
