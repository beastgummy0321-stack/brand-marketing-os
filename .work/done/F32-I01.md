# Objective
A hand-launched internal campaign becomes `live` when launch readback binds its campaign node, and `ended` when its planned end date has passed. No migration: `ads_campaign.status` already allows `live`/`ended` (`20260913000001_ads_tables.sql:28`).

# Done
1. `repository.bind_campaign` (`app/ads/repository.py:1153`): the same UPDATE also sets `status='live'` when the row's `status='ready'` (SQL `CASE`, no second statement). Any other status is left alone. `bind_group`/`bind_ad` unchanged.
2. `runtime._health` (`app/ads/runtime.py:22`, the `plan_date_passed` branch at `:240`): when the campaign's `status='live'` and `end_date <= today`, one UPDATE sets `status='ended'` (org/brand/campaign scoped, `origin='internal'`). Never touches `closed`; never enqueues a close-out. The alert text stays.
3. Tests, red first on master: in `test_ads_handoff.py` the launch-readback path that confirms a campaign node (`_finish` calling `bind_campaign`, see `handoff_check.py:437`) ends with `ads_campaign.status='live'`; a group-only readback leaves `ready`; a second readback on the live campaign keeps `live`. In `test_ads_runtime.py` a live campaign with a past `end_date` is `ended` after `_health`, and a `closed` one is untouched. `test_ads_external_sync.py:792` and `test_ads_outcome.py:114` call `bind_campaign` directly; adjust only if their fixtures assert on `status`.
