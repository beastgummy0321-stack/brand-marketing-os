# Objective
The ads backend asks one function which platform day is complete, takes deferral dates as org calendar dates, serves the limits its
screens quote, and checks ad-set dates, uploads and the one-sentence create with the shared rules (feature Contracts K and I;
audit W11, W12; triage #11, #12; planning #4／#12, #13; slots (d); frontend-ads F5 backend half; drill-11 R3 N2, R6 A9 (group
dates), B1, F11; controls c1-08, c2-02; faults F11-empty). Today there are three "yesterdays" (account zone with a two-day
fallback, org zone, the read's finish moment), a closed campaign's 「近 14 天」 is anchored to today, the AI month starts at UTC,
the screens restate 31／7／30／5 days and items, an ad set can run outside its campaign, an empty upload is told 「超過 50 MiB 或沒有
內容，請另建批次」, and 一句話建立 drops platform and cap.

# Done
1. `app/ads/coverage.py` gains `last_complete_day(conn, org, brand, campaign_id=None)` per Contract K (campaign's ad-account zone
   via `settings.last_complete_day(zone=…)`; brand-level = the earliest over bound accounts; unknown = `settings.last_complete_day(org)
   - 1 day`). `triage._account_cutoff` goes; triage, `period_grades`, `ab_pairs`, `outcome._coverage`, `review_evidence`,
   `review_monitor` windows, `workspace_reads._totals`, `home_overview._window` and the tracked-zero cutoff in `external_sync` call
   it. `external_sync` records a clean range clipped to `today_in(account zone at finish) - 1`; `connections._proven` stops
   clipping on read.
2. Workspace totals (R3 N2): `_totals` uses `coverage.window(campaign, cutoff, TOTALS_DAYS)` and serves `totals.window {start, end,
   days, label}` (「近 14 天」 while the window ends at the cutoff, 「結束前 14 天」 when the end date cut it).
3. Instants through `db.now_utc()` (`strategy.py:627`, `launch_settings.py:424`, `manual_settings.py:159`,
   `external_routes.py:72`); AI month = `settings.org_month_start` in `review_rounds._reserve` and `asset_suggestions._reserve`.
4. Deferral: `change_routes` and `review_routes` decide bodies accept `revisit_on: "YYYY-MM-DD"` (`> today_for_org`, else 400
   「暫緩請選明天或之後的日期。」), stored as `settings.org_day_start(revisit_on)`; `revisit_at` stays accepted until F50-I12 moves the
   senders and deletes it.
5. Limits served on `GET …/rules`: `limits {review_days {min, max}, review_cadence_default, campaign_default_days, copy_items_max,
   upload_max_mb}` from named constants (`review_monitor.DAYS_RANGE`, `review_monitor.DEFAULT_CADENCE_DAYS`,
   `brief.DEFAULT_PERIOD_DAYS` — both 30-day defaults at `brief.py:466,551` read it — `change_control_data.COPY_ITEMS_MAX`,
   `input_rules.UPLOAD_CAPS["ad_asset"]`); the refusals that enforce them quote the same constants.
6. Ad-set dates: `plan.update_group`／`create_group` refuse a start or end outside the campaign's dates with
   `input_rules.date_within(…, "投放組日期", "活動期間")` → 400 code `group_dates_outside` with that sentence.
7. Uploads: `media_probe`, `user_assets`, `user_assets_repository`, `user_assets_routes` read `UPLOAD_CAPS["ad_asset"]` and refuse
   through `check_upload` (empty 「「{name}」是空的檔案，請換一個。」 vs oversize); `user_assets_repository`'s date-order copy calls
   `input_rules.date_order`; 「补」 at `asset_suggestions.py:364` becomes 「補」.
8. 一句話建立 (R6 B1) fills platform and cap defaults like the structured path; model-parsed `budget`／`platform`／`audience` are
   `field_sources = "parsed"`, never `user` (planning #4／#12). Readers that branch on `"user"` (e.g. the audience default
   `brief.py:607-614`) treat `parsed` as not the user's pick.
9. `test_clock_rule.py` and `test_input_rules_gate.py`: delete every allowlist entry naming F50-I05. Tests: an account in
   `America/Los_Angeles` and an org in Taipei give one cutoff to triage, totals, home and connections; a missing account zone falls
   back two days; a closed campaign's totals window ends at its end date with numbers; `revisit_on` today → 400, tomorrow → stored
   org midnight; `GET …/rules` serves `limits` equal to the constants; a group dated before the campaign → 400; an empty and a 51 MB
   upload give their own sentences; a sentence-path create carries platform and cap and marks parsed fields `parsed`.
