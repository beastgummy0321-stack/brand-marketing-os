# Objective

One 文案 round stops being three pools (5 bodies ＋ 5 headlines ＋ 5 descriptions that someone must
combine) and becomes **5 complete sets**, each a usable ad on its own: `{body, headline,
description}`. This is `ARCHITECTURE.md` §5.6 and it voids every 2026-09-09 copy decision — the
per-length body counts 5／3／2, 建議組合, and the separate headline/description pools. `copy_length`
survives and still only picks the 主文 band.

The seam is in this issue on purpose: `app/schedule/state.py::_copy_summary` reads the same
`body_json`, and `app/tests/test_workbench.py`／`test_schedule.py` drive both sides of it
(`ARCHITECTURE.md` §2 — those files are legacy, an issue that touches them carries
`legacy_migration`). Splitting it would leave the gate red between two issues.

## Where the count lives (planner ruling 2026-09-12, re-queue of the blocked round)

`BODY_BANDS` becomes 2-tuples `(lo, hi)` and **`COPY_SET_COUNT = 5` in `app/workbench/validate.py`
is the only place the number 5 is written**. Keeping the count inside the band would restate
「一輪 5 組」 three times and contradict §5.6 (`copy_length` decides the band only). That makes
`prompts.py` part of this issue — it is in `touch` now; the previous round was blocked because it
was not.

**Every reader of `BODY_BANDS`／`BAND_LABELS` today (grep of `app/`, 2026-09-12) — all three must
be updated in this issue, there are no others:**

- `app/workbench/validate.py:76` — `check_copy`, rewritten below.
- `app/workbench/prompts.py:53-56` — `copy_prompt`'s `length_line`, rewritten below.
- `app/workbench/jobs.py:116-117` — `_shape_hint`, rewritten below.

(`BAND_LABELS` has no other reader; no test asserts on `BODY_BANDS` or on the length line's text.)
