   (`<Button>開始做素材 →</Button>`, `disabled={dispatching}`, label 「交棒中…」 while dispatching, the existing

   `step.id === "make_assets"` (`<Button>前往素材 →</Button>` via `requestTabChange("assets")`, hint 「工作檯做好的文案會出現在
   素材頁的格子裡」). Those two steps are then not rendered inside the 進階 fold; the other steps (`add_ad`, `publish`, strategy
   ones) keep today's in-fold `StepButton`／`NextStepButton`. Imported campaigns: unchanged bar.

   entry (`materialModes` itself is untouched — `MaterialTaskEditor` and existing copy tasks still render). A saved copy-mode

3. `AssetsTab.tsx`: pass `draft={isDraftCampaign(props.campaign, null)}` (import from `./workspace/WorkspaceHead`).
   `review/MaterialStepPage.tsx` keeps its call unchanged (live campaigns keep four cards).

   unchanged. `MaterialTasksPanel.test.tsx`: `draft` shows three cards without 「只準備新文案」; without `draft` four cards.
   `AssetsTab.test.tsx`: a draft campaign's fold shows three source cards.
