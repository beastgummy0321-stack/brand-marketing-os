# Objective

With many briefs the workbench rail grows past the viewport and the third group 工單 is pushed off
screen: strict acceptance measured its heading at y=1812 (1024x768) and y=1698 (1920x1080) with 12
dispatched briefs, page height 3961 / 3653 px. F01-T04's human check requires all three rail groups
(開始生成 / 派工任務 / 工單) visible without scrolling the page. ARCHITECTURE.md §5.2 says 派工任務 is
"always visible, never an inbox that collapses", so no cap and no "more" link: the rail keeps its
height inside the viewport and the two list groups scroll inside the rail.

# Done

All changes are in `BriefList.tsx`; the shared `.workspace-subnav` class (also used by 品牌智庫, 設定)
is not edited.

- The `<nav className="workspace-subnav" ...>` gets the extra class `max-h-[calc(100vh-112px)]`:
  `className="workspace-subnav max-h-[calc(100vh-112px)]"`. 112px = the rail's sticky `top: 88px`
  (`.workspace-subnav` in `frontend/src/styles/`) + a 24px bottom gap. Precedent for arbitrary
  max-height with overflow: `frontend/src/components/schedule/PlanComparison.tsx:43`.
- The 開始生成 label and its four buttons stay direct children of the nav, unchanged; they never
  scroll.
- Everything between the 派工任務 label and the 工單 label (the loading skeleton, the error
  `EmptyState`, and the three `SourceGroup`s) moves into one wrapper
  `<div className="flex min-h-0 flex-col gap-1 overflow-y-auto">`. The 派工任務 `<p>` label stays
  outside, above it.
- Everything after the 工單 label (全部 list with its 已關閉 `<details>`, and 已採用 list) moves into
  a second wrapper with the identical className. The 工單 `<p>` label stays outside, above it.
- Use exactly that className string on both wrappers (no `flex-1`: the default `flex: 0 1 auto`
  lets both regions shrink in proportion to their content once the nav hits its max-height, and
  keeps their natural height when it does not).
- Nothing else changes: row markup, labels, empty-state sentences, filtering, `aria-pressed`.
