# Outcome

Deleting an archived brand that has a brief linked to one of its products succeeds, and deleting an
archived product that a brief points at succeeds too. Today both fail on
`workbench_brief.product_id REFERENCES product(id)` (`supabase/migrations/20260909000001_workbench_baseline.sql:27`):
`cleanup.delete_all` runs registered modules in registration order, 品牌智庫 always registers before
素材工作檯 (`app/workbench/repository.py:17` imports `bi_repo`), and 品牌智庫 deletes `product`
(`BRAND_CHILD_TABLES`, last entry) before 素材工作檯 deletes its briefs. The product path never
deletes briefs at all (素材工作檯 registers no product cleanup).

# Acceptance

- An archived brand with a product and a brief whose `product_id` is that product is deleted by
  `bi_repo.delete_brand` without error; no `workbench_brief` row of that brand remains.
- An archived product that a brief points at is deleted by `bi_repo.delete_product` without error;
  the brief remains, with `product_id` NULL (a brief's product is optional, ARCHITECTURE.md §5.4).
- Existing brand-delete, product-delete and cleanup-registry tests stay green; the isolation trio
  stays green.
