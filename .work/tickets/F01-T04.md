# Outcome

The workbench opens on 「我要做什麼」, not on a queue. The left rail carries 開始生成 (four content
types), 派工任務 grouped by source, and 工單 (全部／已採用); the work area holds either the new-brief
form or the selected brief. `docs/design-system.md` §5.2 describes this, and the layout is the
existing `.workspace-split` pattern - no new pattern is invented.

# Acceptance

- A brand with no briefs still shows all three 派工任務 groups, each reading 「尚無任務」.
- 開始生成 → 文案 opens a form with 文案 ticked, a length picker, an optional product, and free text;
  ticking 圖片提示詞 adds its count field to the same form; submitting creates one brief.
- A dispatched brief shows a 文案 card and an 圖片提示詞 card even before either has been generated;
  the image-prompt card's count is prefilled 10 for 彈性格式 and 3 otherwise.
- A dispatched brief's image-prompt card shows ① 主圖提示詞 and ② 變體提示詞 copy buttons in that
  order; a manual brief's shows ③ 探索提示詞 and ② 變體提示詞.
- The 生成圖片 button is grey, disabled and labelled as a reserved seat; nothing on the page says the
  feature is refused or unavailable.
- Closed briefs are still reachable from 工單／全部 and can still be re-opened.
- No 短／中／長 tabs anywhere; a copy card shows the brief's one length with its hint sentence.
- Gates in `docs/design-system.md` §9 pass, including the width-overflow e2e check.
