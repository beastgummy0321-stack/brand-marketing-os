# Outcome

檔期規劃 becomes 投放中心: a Campaign is a real object that carries why it exists (`goal_text`),
what it is for (`objective`) and how it will be judged (`success_criterion`), and the whole loop
closes on rows instead of JSON blobs — strategy → delivery plan (Campaign → 通路 → 投放組 → 廣告)
→ one 任務包 into 素材工作檯 → publish through a channel adapter (manual or simulated) + bind →
daily outcome rows attributed by the bound entity → 結案 judges against the campaign's own success
criterion → one learning row in 品牌智庫 → the next campaign's strategy reads it. Five tabs exist at
minimal fidelity; the old four-stage page is gone, with no parallel run.

This is feature 1 of 3 (phasing A). Feature 2 = 投放中心首頁的工作佇列＋建議／採用／為什麼／更新鈕.
Feature 3 = 發布前檢查／批次操作／Inspector／完整 Campaign 清單／圖級細緻度. The prototype is the
shared visual reference for all three; anything it shows that belongs to feature 2 or 3 stays out.

# Decisions

Source: the user's rulings 2026-09-12 (`.harness/scratch/ads-v2-summary.md`, 23 items, quoted below
in their words where the wording carries the decision) plus the two the user delegated to the
planner. Architecture-level consequences are in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §0, §2, §3, §4 and §6 — an issue
follows those, this file does not repeat them.

## The user's 23 rulings (verbatim summary, 2026-09-12)

1. **平台寫入留接口，這版做手動發布。** 一般路徑「發布 Campaign」顯示「自動發布未接上」；右上「⋯」
   進階選單有「手動發布」＝帶複製鈕的清單 → 使用者到平台後台建好 → 「配對」→ 投放中。
2. **證據由程式算、判斷交外部 LLM。** 程式把花費資料整理成一張數字表，LLM 只回最多 5 條建議；
   信心度＝樣本等級（低／中／高），永遠不顯示百分比。（建議本身是 F04；這條約束在 F03 就生效：
   任何數字都不進 prompt 之外的地方編造。）
3. **學習層最小版。** 用現成 `learning_ledger`；結案與「採用」各寫一條（證據、樣本、來源活動、
   期間）；分級死規則：1 個活動＝已觀察、≥2 同方向＝可能成立、已驗證＝人工勾或 ≥3；建議待人工
   核准併入下一期，放超過 7 天在建立下一個 Campaign 時自動採納並標「自動採納」（自動採納是 F04）。
4. **預算搬進投放中心，不再在設定頁。** 損益平衡 CPA＝售價−單位成本−運費；目標 CPA＝×0.7。
   沒投放過：日預算＝目標 CPA × 每天想要的購買數（最低 2／推薦 5／積極 10），標「冷啟動估算」；
   投放過：近 30 天 CPA 中位數＋上一期 ±20%，三情境 ×0.6／×1／×1.6。全部可手改，AI 只是預填。
5. **本月預算卡**：上限（選填，投放中心設）／建議／已使用；按通路分列，未接上的通路寫「未接上」。
   （F03 只做「這個 Campaign 的預算」；跨 Campaign 的本月預算卡是 F04 的首頁工作。）
6. **不寫死平台。** 通路可以是 Meta、Google Ads、TikTok、蝦皮、momo 等；資料模型是
   Campaign → 通路 → 投放組 → 廣告，平台原生名詞只在需要時顯示；這版只有 Meta 接上。
7. **預算分配只到投放組層**；廣告層只顯示實際花費占比。
8. **不強制站內生素材。** Ready 兩個來源：工作檯核准整包（自動）或使用者勾「素材已就位」（手動，
   記誰何時）。直接在平台後台照追蹤名稱建的廣告，配對後照樣追蹤。工作檯「成品上傳」槽延後。
9. **採用建議**先偵測權杖有沒有改預算權限（純讀取），沒有就彈窗寫清楚哪一組改到多少。（F04）
10. **發布前檢查全部可略過**，手動發布沒有硬擋。（檢查本身是 F05；F03 不做任何硬擋。）
11. **資料拉取**每 6 小時一次＋手動更新鈕（10 分鐘冷卻）＋「最後更新 N 分鐘前」。（更新鈕是 F04；
    F03 沿用既有的每日拉取，成效分頁顯示資料時間。）
12. **改名**：檔期規劃→投放中心、檔期→Campaign；文案用「行銷駕駛艙」。AI 投放顧問入口保留、
    顯示「未發布」。「留言討論」不做。
13. **結案**：結束後 7 天自動結案並寫學習，可提前手動；花費按綁定的平台實體直接歸屬，
    舊「重疊分帳」退場。
14. 相似案例只算同品牌；「預估影響」用 CPA 不變的算術推估並標「推估」；ROAS 主數字保守值、
    樂觀值小字。
15. 採用建議＝一筆 Action（走核准）＋一條學習，不再寫成品牌規則；使用者打字的回饋才是規則。
16. 學習不是品牌事實：不寫進品牌事實；超過 180 天或來源商品換價 → 標「需要重新驗證」；
    「已驗證」要同期對照或 ≥3 個活動，手動勾可。
17. 提示詞按任務取用：策略任務拿品牌事實（附更新時間）＋今天有效的商品價格（過期標示）＋學習＋
    壓縮歷史表；建議任務只拿數字表；「查看策略依據」列出用到的資料。
18. 每個 Action 可撤銷（反向 Action）；7 天自動採納只改下一個活動草稿。
19. 通路介面兩個實作：手動（清單＋配對）、模擬（演練／驗收用）；自動接 Meta 預留第三個。
20. Campaign 物件帶「為什麼做」（那句話）、目標、成功條件（指標＋目標值＋依據）；結案對成功條件判。
21. Action 表：改了哪個節點、改前改後、誰核准、同步狀態；取代預算版本表。
22. 少碰別的模組：工作檯「成品上傳」延後；設定頁只拿掉預算卡；meta 只加頻率／權限／Pixel 三個讀取
    （三個都是 F04／F05，F03 一個都不加）。
23. 分期 A：F1 薄閉環（五分頁陽春）→ F2 首頁佇列＋建議／採用／為什麼＋更新鈕 → F3 發布前檢查／
    批次／Inspector／清單頁／圖級細緻度。原型是三期共同參考圖。

## The two the planner decided (delegated by the user, 2026-09-12)

- **New managed module `ads`, not a reshape of `schedule`.** Reason and boundary: `ARCHITECTURE.md`
  §2, §4, §6.1. `schedule` keeps its package because 經營概況 still reads its 判讀 surfaces; what
  retires in F03 is its page, its route and its sidebar entry. No issue in F03 edits `app/schedule/`
  except to delete the frontend and its route.
- **`learning_ledger` is revived where it is** (brand_intelligence, legacy), with
  `record_learning` / `list_learnings` added to `app/brand_intelligence/repository.py` and `ads`
  calling those — `ARCHITECTURE.md` §3. An ads-owned `ads_learning` table was rejected: learning is
  brand knowledge shown in 品牌智庫. The other four dead tables are not touched in this feature.

## The golden path this feature must close

brand → offering → goal (the brief) → strategy → delivery plan → pack → artifacts → publish
(manual or simulated) + bind → outcome → verdict against `success_criterion` → learning →
the next campaign's strategy reads the learning. Every issue's `# Objective` names which link it
serves, where its input comes from, where its output goes, what trace it leaves, and whether it is
core or pluggable (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §0).

## Shape decisions an issue must not re-invent

- **Tables** (`ads` owns all five, one migration file, `<timestamp>_ads_*.sql`): `ads_campaign`
  (goal/objective/success criterion/status/dates/budget), `ads_delivery_group` (channel key, role,
  daily budget, binding columns), `ads_ad` (coordinates, binding columns, readiness), `ads_action`
  (every platform-bound change), `ads_outcome_daily` (one row per bound entity per day).
  The 通路 level is a column on the group, not a table (§6.2).
- **Binding lives on the row**, not in a map table: `external_id` / `external_name` /
  `external_status` / `local_state` columns on `ads_delivery_group` and `ads_ad`. `channel_entity_map`
  belongs to `schedule` and is not read or written by `ads`.
- **Matching is by exact name** through `app/meta/repository.py`'s `campaign_ids_by_exact_name` /
  `ad_set_ids_by_exact_name` / `ad_ids_by_exact_name`; the failure wording rules of
  `app/schedule/binding.py` (no-account-bound message wins alone; per-node reasons; dedup; one
  message for the batch) are reused as behaviour, re-implemented small in `ads`, never imported.
- **Outcome ingest** reads `meta_entity_spend` only through `app/meta/repository.py`
  (`daily_entity_spend`); the simulated adapter writes `ads_outcome_daily` directly. No module
  writes another module's table.
- **Workbench handoff** keeps `ARCHITECTURE.md` §5.4 intact: `submit_pack`, one pack per campaign,
  one 素材組 per 廣告, `required_outputs = {"copy": 1, "image_prompt": 1}`. `source_kind` stays
  `"schedule_node"` (the DB token for 「上游派工」, CHECK-constrained and tied to §5.7's 變體模式);
  the human-readable origin is a string the upstream passes in, not something the workbench derives.
  Readiness comes back through a workbench public entry — never a read of `workbench_*` tables.
- **Prompts**: at most three new department files in F03 — `app/departments/ads/brief_parse.md`,
  `strategy.md`, `delivery_plan.md` (the suggestion task is F04). No `app/methodology/**` change.
  Every LLM call goes through `app/llm/gateway.py::run_task` and leaves its `llm_task` row, whose
  `knowledge_item_ids_json` is what 「查看策略依據」 shows.
- **Routes**: `/brands/:brandId/ads` (投放中心, campaign list + 建立 Campaign) and
  `/brands/:brandId/ads/campaigns/:campaignId` (the five-tab workspace, tab in the query string);
  `/brands/:brandId/schedule` is deleted. `docs/design-system.md` §3's route count and §5.1 are
  amended by the issue that does the rename; `CONTEXT.md`'s 檔期 vocabulary entry is amended in the
  same issue.
- **Frontend**: `docs/design-system.md` rules as written (PageHeader → PageTabs → one §5 pattern,
  every number captioned, honest empties, tokens only, kit components, 1024–1920). Visual reference:
  `.harness/scratch/prototype/ads-v2/` (`screen-overview.js`, `screen-new.js`, `screen-strategy.js`,
  `screen-delivery.js`, `screen-assets.js`, `screen-performance.js`, `screen-summary.js`,
  `screen-manual.js`) — reference for layout and wording, not a component to port; anything in it
  belonging to feature 2 or 3 (work queue, suggestions, preflight panel, batch bar, 查看全部 list,
  inspector polish) is out of F03.

# Acceptance

One walk, on a restarted backend, with the `simulated` adapter and test data (the system is
unpublished; `supabase db reset` and rebuild is allowed):

1. 側欄「投放中心」 opens the campaign list; a brand with nothing shows an honest empty and one
   「建立 Campaign」 entry.
2. 建立 Campaign: one sentence, start and end date, optional constraints → a draft Campaign exists
   with `goal_text`, a derived `objective`, a `success_criterion` (metric, target, basis) and the
   ids of the brand/product rows it used; every derived field is editable and labelled 系統帶入.
3. 策略: one recommended strategy with the 新客／再行銷／探索 split and three budget scenarios;
   with no history every scenario carries 冷啟動估算 and 「哪些是推估」 says so; 查看策略依據 lists
   the brand facts, the offering price with its effective date, and the learnings used.
4. 採用策略 writes the delivery plan as rows — one channel (Meta), 投放組 with daily budgets, 廣告
   with coordinates and tracking names; editing a group budget changes the campaign total; the ad
   level shows no budget field.
5. 素材: one pack lands in 素材工作檯 with one 素材組 per 廣告 and an origin line pointing back;
   核准整包 turns that ad 已就位（工作檯）; another ad is ticked 素材已就位 by hand and records who
   and when.
6. 發布: the normal path says 自動發布未接上; 手動發布 gives the copyable checklist and binds by
   exact name; the simulated adapter publishes and produces bound entities plus daily outcome rows.
7. 成效: daily spend / purchases / CPA / ROAS (conservative main, optimistic in the caption) with
   every number captioned; before publish it reads 還沒有成效資料, never 0.
8. 結案 (manual now, automatic at end+7d proven by a test): the verdict compares the outcome with
   `success_criterion` and is allowed to be 看不出; one learning row is written at 已觀察 with its
   evidence, its sample and its source campaign, and shows up in 品牌智庫.
9. The next campaign's strategy prompt includes that learning and 查看策略依據 names it.
10. Every change that was meant to reach a platform (publish, bind, budget edit after publish) has
    an `ads_action` row with before/after/who/sync state, and the undo of one of them writes a
    reverse action.
