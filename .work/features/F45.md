# Outcome
Drill 10's three blocking defects (report §4.1) and their same-root siblings disappear from their upstream causes:
1. **B1 + M1 + O1 (root cause 5, 10's offer part; drill 9 #9)** — a sell-way is *base* (one product, no quantity) or *composed*
   (base sell-way × qty, any products); a composed sell-way's empty price / list price / cost is derived from its bases' own numbers
   and labelled 推算 everywhere it shows; a sell-way can say 「這個賣法免運」; the offer sentence carries no product name.
2. **B2 (root cause 4 on this path)** — the pause proposal page reads the ad itself; every 「停掉／暫停」 entry lands on a working page
   for an active, an already-paused and a deleted ad; the home queue only adds the position.
3. **B3 + S1 + S2 (root cause 3)** — every confirmation path stores its refusal; a manual copy edit is checked at once; a slot reads
   「處理中」 only while a job for it runs, otherwise 「等你：〈next action〉」 or 卡住 with the reason; a photo-only brand completes a
   material set without an image prompt.

# Decisions
User-confirmed (2026-10-02): the three rulings above (base / composed sell-ways with derived numbers labelled 推算, the free-shipping
flag, the nameless offer sentence; the pause page reads the ad and covers review, read-back and 成效 entries with already-paused and
unknown states; refusal stored by the shared confirmation, immediate re-check, state from facts, S2). Test data is rebuilt — no
legacy backfill beyond what the migration needs.

Settled contracts (one authority each; workers read the section their issue names):
- Sell-way storage, facade, derivation, offers, step-4 / bundle API shapes, the migration's legacy rule:
  `docs/architecture/intake.md` 「F45 (ruled 2026-10-02): base and composed sell-ways」.
- Pause view (Contract P), pause page, refusal storage and immediate re-check, slot states (Contract W), photo-only sets, picker
  `derived_fields`: `docs/architecture/ads.md` §6.49.

Agent choices (recorded there; restated only where they cut across issues):
- `product_component.product_id` is renamed `base_sell_way_id` so no reader keeps the old meaning silently.
- Composed price may be left empty (prototype) → `selling_price` nullable for composed rows; the save-time component checks make a
  NULL effective price unreachable through the product writers.
- Free-shipping sentence 「免運」 (base) / 「這組免運」 (composed), from the prototype; both keys use 「優惠價 NT$X（原價 NT$Y）」.
- A composed sell-way needs Σqty ≥ 2 (one component × 1 is refused: 「…數量至少 2」). Per-sale cap and landing page sit in the
  editor's folded 進階 area (prototype).
- A store-wide free-shipping policy that already covers a sell-way plus its own 免運 flag → the offer list carries one
  free_shipping sentence, the sell-way's own (「免運」／「這組免運」); never both (lead, 2026-10-02).
- Same-product combinations live on the product's step 4; the bundle page needs bases of at least two products (keeps `kind`).
- 「成效頁 entry」 = a 「暫停」 button on every active ad row of the 成效 tab (prototype `perfPage`), plus reviews started there.
- The pause set does not carry `review_id`; `proposal_reference` stays copy-only.
- The slot copy editor shows one field per non-empty copy field (only 內文 → exactly the prototype's single textarea).
- Out of scope: the prototype's carousel-format note on a ready slot (root cause 2; F46-I03 adds `format_note` to the same slots
  payload — F45 does not), job staleness / dead workers (root cause 7),
  Q2 deferral re-entry, everything else in report §4.2.

Migration: `supabase/migrations/20261004000010_brand_intelligence_sell_way_base.sql` (F45 owns 2026100400001x). Lead applies it to
