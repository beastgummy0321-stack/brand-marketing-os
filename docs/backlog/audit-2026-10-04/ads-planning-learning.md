## Answers to the brief's five questions (evidence in the tables)

**(a) Budget totals.** Seven places compute "the money" for one campaign and they do not share one rule:
`plan.adopt_strategy` daily_total = `round(budget_planned/days)` (plan.py:360) → `budget.split` per group;
`repository._budget_total` = Σgroup.daily_budget × campaign days (repository.py:38-53, re-implemented in TS at
`frontend/src/lib/adsApi.ts:315-326`); `strategy._apply_amounts` / `budget.reconcile_campaign` = `budget.split(budget_planned)`
per role, a PERIOD number recomputed on every read (budget.py:266-277); `StrategyTab.amountOf` = `Math.round(total×pct/100)`
(StrategyTab.tsx:250-251, third split rule, no remainder); `budget._with_budget_cap` cap row daily=`round(cap/days)`,
total=cap (budget.py:199-203); `triage_capacity.capacity_view` daily = Σ platform owner budget_amount (triage_capacity.py:46);
`month_budget.calculate` ongoing = Σ platform entity daily×days (month_budget.py:181-203). After a non-divisible total,
a cap change, a group daily edit, or a date edit on a `ready` campaign they disagree (rows 1–4).

**(b) Who "enters learning" / is 留下.** Not one rule, five: `learning_rank.rank` (closeout coordinate learnings, no sample gate);
`triage.grade_window` (triage, cleanup, period_grades → month review AND settle learnings; gate 3×cap or ≥10 results/≥7 days);
`outcome.sample_bucket` (closeout campaign verdict: <10 purchases or <3 days ⇒ 看不出); `experiments.DEFAULT_RULES`
(A/B: 10 each / 7 days / 10%); `month_summary._verdict` (monthly met/missed, no gate at all). R1 covers #1 vs #2; rows 5–8 are the rest.

**(c) Numbers in planner/strategy prose.** Money/percent amounts: program (`budget.split`, `_apply_amounts`). Everything
textual is LLM and stored/shown unchecked: strategy `rationale/why/unknowns/learning_goals` (strategy.py:289-311) — the
department file *asks* the model to copy 每筆成本 and 「第 N 群」 numbers into `rationale` (strategy.md:29-35); `alternatives`
is accepted as any list (strategy.py:309, frontend type `unknown[]`); plan `name`/`scene` only length-checked; brief
`audience_hint`/`notes` flow into the copy brief 「目標受眾／重點訊息」 (assets.py:203-206) and the handoff audience text
(launch_view.py:94). The delivery prompt states a cap (ads ≤ photos) that no program check enforces (delivery_plan.md:27-28).

**(d) Product facts reaching each planner.** brief: brand-level claims/rules/facts via `context_compiler.compile(conn, brand_id)`
(product never passed even on the structured path, brief.py:450 vs 469) + product names/prices. strategy: brand facts,
knowledge-product facts, sell-way prices, customer groups, ads-track learnings, ranked coordinate learnings, calendar — NO
claims (approved/forbidden), NO rules, NO offer sentences, NO media capability. plan: allocation, calendar, ranked learnings,
customer groups, material counts, offer sentences, vocabulary — NO product name, NO facts, NO price, NO claims/rules.
Stored but read by no planner: forbidden/approved claims (product-level; `product_onboarding.py:984`), brand rules, visual
learnings (`source_track="visual"`, only `workbench/looks.py:188` reads), previous strategy `learning_goals`
(written strategy.py:310, shown StrategyTab.tsx:363, never read back), `learning_ledger.product_id` (never written by any
product writer, see row 9).

**(e) Meta structure in shared files.** The "normalized" entity vocabulary is Meta's raw vocabulary: `native_kind` campaign/adset/ad
(period_grades.py:75,97; repository.py:713; triage_scale.py:60-85) and `native_status` ACTIVE/PAUSED/ADSET_PAUSED/
CAMPAIGN_PAUSED (ad_status.py:13 says so itself; ab_pairs.py:291 emits `{"status": "PAUSED"}` as a change payload;
change_control_data.py:261 compares to it). `channels.default_channel_key` falls back to `CHANNELS[0]` = meta for a brand
with nothing connected (channels.py:67) so `plan.adopt_strategy` stamps meta on groups. `brief_parse.md:34` hard-codes the
five platform keys in prompt text. The machine check `test_ads_no_platform_literal.py` scans only the substring "meta", so
none of these can trip it.
