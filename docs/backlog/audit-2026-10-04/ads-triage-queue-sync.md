## Answers to the brief's questions (evidence index)

(a) Vocabularies and interpreters: campaign status (`ads_tables.sql:27-28`; interpreters #4 #5 #16; `judgment.py:96-131`; `campaign_stage.py:20-33`; `campaignWords.ts`), ad `native_status` (#1, `_closed_block triage.py:212-235`, `triage_pause._state:44-47`), sync job (#2, `runtime._sources:39-65`, `home_overview._sync_block:583-599`), section sync state → `campaign_sync_state` → `campaign_data_status` (`repository.py:448-586`; R6 drift above), queue kinds (#5 #6), handoff item / action states (#8 #9; `launch_view._STATES:23-30`; read-time `stale` override `change_control_data.py:383-389`), change-set status (`change_control.py`), connections source states (`connections.py:55-57,224-235`), delivery (`workspace_reads.py:136-148`).
